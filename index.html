About Us

One club, three ways in.

The club is broad on purpose. You can be casual, competitive, research-focused, or all three — and you can change your mind in November.

Explore

Learn the world properly, not just the trivia version of it.

  • World, human, and physical geography
  • Earth science systems
  • Geodata, maps, OSINT, and analysis

Compete

Take what you know into a room with a buzzer or a panel of judges.

  • GeoGuessr practice and in-club leagues
  • IAC / Geography Bowl rounds
  • JSHS and The Earth Prize

Research

Pick a question about where you live and actually answer it.

  • Local questions worth asking
  • Public data and real evidence
  • A paper, poster, or presentation
